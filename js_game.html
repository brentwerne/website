Falling Block Game

Score Count: 0

Rules of the game:

You are the red square, the following blocks are the blue squares. Your player can move left and right and your player can rap around the boundary similar to the tunnels in pac-man. If you collide with a blue block the game ends and your score will display. You get a point added to your score everytime a block passes your player and disappears of the screen. If you resize or refresh the window during a game the game will restart.
